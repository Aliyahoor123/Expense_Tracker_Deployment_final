Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects when you use our personal expense tracking application ("Service"), how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide directly when using the Service, including your name, email address, and password (stored in hashed form) when you register, and the expense records you create — amount, category, date, and description.

We do not collect payment card numbers, bank account details, or other sensitive financial account information — Spendly only stores the expense entries you choose to log manually.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to operate the Service on your behalf: authenticating your account, displaying your expense history, and generating summaries and breakdowns of your spending by category and time period.

We do not use your expense data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in the application's database and retained for as long as your account remains active. Passwords are never stored in plain text — they are hashed before being saved. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal expense data with third-party advertisers or data brokers. The Service may rely on limited third-party infrastructure (such as font or hosting providers) strictly to operate the application; these providers do not receive access to your expense records.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us through the appropriate support channel for the Service.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of Spendly after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
